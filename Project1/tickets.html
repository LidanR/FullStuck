Planning Tips for First-Time Visitors:

  1. Review the Animals & Habitats page to prioritize exhibits you wish to see.
  2. Arrive 15 minutes before opening to catch the 10:30 AM penguin morning feeding.
  3. Wear comfortable closed-toe walking shoes suitable for outdoor paved and gravel nature paths.
