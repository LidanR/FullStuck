Vulnerable

African Lion

Panthera leo • African Savannah

The apex predator of open grasslands. Lions are the only cats that live in family prides, cooperating to hunt large prey and defend their expansive territories.

Learn more on Wikipedia ↗
Endangered

African Bush Elephant

Loxodonta africana • Savannah & Forest

The world's largest living terrestrial mammal. Known for complex social intelligence, deep familial bonds, and ecosystem-shaping keystone foraging behaviors.

Learn more on Wikipedia ↗
Vulnerable

Humboldt Penguin

Spheniscus humboldti • Coastal Marine

Native to South American Pacific coasts, these agile aquatic swimmers feed on schooling anchovies and thrive within our climate-controlled salt-water cove exhibit.

Learn more on Wikipedia ↗
Endangered

Red Panda

Ailurus fulgens • Himalayan Montane

Arboreal acrobats with reddish-brown fur, ringed tails, and false thumbs tailored for grasping bamboo. Our sanctuary supports community forestry conservation in Nepal.

Learn more on Wikipedia ↗
Stable

Golden Poison Frog

Phyllobates terribilis • Tropical Rainforest

Sporting bright aposematic warning coloration, these tiny amphibians highlight the intricate biodiversity found in dense neo-tropical rainforest understories.

Learn more on Wikipedia ↗
Vulnerable

Reticulated Giraffe

Giraffa camelopardalis • Acacia Woodland

Tallest mammal on Earth with iconic polygon-patterned coats and prehensile tongues reaching up to 45 centimeters to browse the highest thorn tree branches.

Learn more on Wikipedia ↗
