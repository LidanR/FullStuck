Welcome to Our Sanctuary

Founded in 1984, City Zoo Sanctuary is dedicated to the highest standards of animal welfare, species preservation, and public education. Our exhibits recreate natural ecosystems ranging from the African savannah to lush tropical rainforests, allowing visitors to connect deeply with wildlife while empowering global rescue initiatives.

Partnered with international organizations including the World Wildlife Fund (WWF), our breeding programs provide vital protection for vulnerable and endangered species worldwide.

Animal of the Month

The African Lion (Panthera leo)

Known as the "King of the Jungle", our resident pride at Savannah Ridge exemplifies the beauty of collective pride dynamics. Through daily behavioral enrichment programs and specialized nutritional care, we ensure our lions thrive in a stimulating, natural environment.

Learn more about lion behavior and ongoing habitat conservation on Wikipedia or meet the pride in person on our Animals & Habitats tour.
